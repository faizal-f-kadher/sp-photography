About

Rooted in Coimbatore, at home anywhere.

From Kodaikanal mornings to Kochi beaches, Chettinad mansions to a busy town bus stand, we go where your story is.

Our work sits between documentary and editorial: most of an album happens without our direction, and the portraits are made with care for light, silk and skin. We work closely with Coimbatore’s bridal makeup artists, rental houses and jewellers, and many of our brides come to us through them.

How we work

From first call to the album

  1. We listen first

    A call or a visit to understand your families, your rituals and the people who matter most on the day.

  2. We plan the light

    Muhurtham times, venues, travel and the golden half hour — mapped before the day so nothing is rushed.

  3. We stay out of the way

    Most of your album happens without our direction. When we do guide, it's brief and for a reason.

  4. We finish it by hand

    Every frame colour-graded for honest skin and true silk, then sequenced into an album and a short film.
