About

Rooted in Coimbatore, at home anywhere.

From Kodaikanal mornings to Kochi beaches, Chettinad mansions to a busy town bus stand, we go where your story is.

Our work sits between documentary and editorial: most of an album happens without our direction, and the portraits are made with care for light, silk and skin. We work closely with Coimbatore’s bridal makeup artists, rental houses and jewellers, and many of our brides come to us through them.
